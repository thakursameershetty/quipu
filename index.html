What Quipus does by itself
  1. Finds

    Quipus reads the request. It gets the service, the member's location and the exact plan. Then it collects the voucher offers for that service near the member.

  2. Understands plain words

    A member writes "my knee still hurts and my doctor wants a scan." Quipus knows that this is a knee MRI. The member does not need the medical name or a billing code. When the request is not clear, Quipus asks.

  3. Filters

    Quipus removes each offer that does not fit: the wrong service, outside the plan's rules, too far, or expired.

  4. Suggests

    Quipus ranks the offers that remain. It puts the best ones in a reply to the member. Each offer shows one service, one location and one price.

Then
  1. 1The reply goes to the member.

    It goes on the channel the member used: email or text. You set the rule: Quipus sends it at once, or an advocate approves it first.

  2. 2The member picks an offer.

    The voucher goes out for that service, at that location, at that price.

  3. 3The case stays open until the visit is done.

    The request, the offers, the pick and the voucher are on the record.

Bill advocacy

The work is done before anyone opens the case.

  1. 01Every document gets read

    Scans, faxes, emails, photos and PDFs. Quipus sorts each one by what it is.

  2. 02The timeline and the ledger get built

    Services, statements and payments go in date order. The ledger sets what each bill demands against what the plan decided.

  3. 03The plan names what the case still needs

    An itemized bill. A corrected claim. A financial assistance form. Each need names who can supply it.

  4. 04The letters get drafted

    Emails and faxes to the provider, the insurer and the member. Each letter asks for a named need.

  5. 05The work gets checked. Then your advocate approves

    A second pass checks the numbers against the documents. Your advocate edits, approves and sends.

FAQ

Questions operations leaders ask first.

Don't see yours? Book a demo and we'll answer it.

Is Quipus an advocacy service?

No. Quipus is software for your advocacy team. Your organization keeps the member relationship and makes each decision.

Does Quipus replace our CRM?

No. Your CRM stays the system of record. Quipus holds the detailed case and writes messages and files back to it.

Can Quipus send anything by itself?

Only what you allow. You set the rule for each kind of request. A reply with care offers can go to the member at once. A dispute letter waits for an advocate. Each message is on the case record.

How do we know a finding is right?

Each amount and date carries the line from its document. Software confirms the line is there. A second pass checks the numbers. Your advocate has the last word.

What happens when Quipus is not sure?

It asks the advocate one clear question, with a recommendation. It does not guess.

How does Quipus pick the offers it suggests?

Coming soon Quipus collects the voucher offers for the service the member asked for. It removes the offers that do not fit the plan, the location or the service. It ranks the rest and suggests the best ones. Your advocate can see each offer that Quipus removed, and why.

How do care vouchers work?

Coming soon The member picks one of the suggested offers. The voucher is for that service, at that location, at that price. The member takes it to the visit.

Who do members hear from?

Your team. Emails go from your mailbox. Texts go from your advocates' numbers.

What if the member already paid?

Quipus checks the payment against the bill and the EOB. If the member paid too much, it drafts the refund request.

How do we start?

With a part of your queue. See the three steps below.
