How it works

The work is done before anyone opens the case.

  1. 01Every document gets read

    Emails, faxes and PDFs are parsed, summarized and filed the moment they arrive.

  2. 02The bill is checked line by line

    Billed, allowed and owed amounts are compared, and every problem is flagged.

  3. 03Fair prices are looked up

    Agents search local rates to set a realistic target for the negotiation.

  4. 04Outreach is drafted

    Emails and faxes to the hospital, insurer and patient, with contacts found on the web.

  5. 05The advocate approves

    One clean summary of the case. Glance, approve, and Quipus sends it.

FAQ

Questions teams ask first.

Don't see yours? Get in touch and we'll answer it.

Who is Quipus for?

TPAs and healthcare advocacy companies whose teams work member billing cases: balance bills, billing errors, surprise bills, appeals and charity care. A retail version for individuals is coming soon.

Does Quipus replace our advocates?

No. Agents read documents, check the bill and draft outreach. Your advocates review and approve every email and fax before it's sent. The point is for each advocate to close more cases, with less time spent on reading and paperwork.

Is Quipus a TPA or an insurance company?

Neither. Quipus is software. It works alongside the plan, the TPA and the advocacy team, and it doesn't adjudicate or pay claims.

How do cases get into Quipus?

From wherever members already send them: your member portal, Salesforce, Zendesk, fax lines and shared email inboxes. New documents are read and filed on the right case as soon as they arrive.

Does Nia make decisions on a case?

No. Nia and the background agents read documents and prepare drafts. A human advocate reviews and approves every email and fax before it's sent.

How do we know a finding is right?

Every fact links back to the page it came from, and the checks that decide a finding are fixed rules, not AI guesses. Results are split into what checked out and what didn't, problems are listed rather than hidden, and Quipus records exactly which documents each result used. Approved actions run in your own systems.

What if the member already paid?

Quipus checks payments against the payer's payment record and the member's receipts, not the provider's portal, which is often out of date. When a member has overpaid, the case is about a refund, and Quipus drafts that request too.
