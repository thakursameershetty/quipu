How it works

The work is done before anyone opens the case.

  1. 01Every document gets read

    Emails, faxes and PDFs are parsed, summarized and filed the moment they arrive.

  2. 02The bill is checked line by line

    Billed, allowed and owed amounts are compared, and every problem is flagged.

  3. 03Fair prices are looked up

    Agents search local rates to set a realistic target for the negotiation.

  4. 04Outreach is drafted

    Emails and faxes to the hospital, insurer and patient, with contacts found on the web.

  5. 05The advocate approves

    One clean summary of the case. Glance, approve, and Quipu sends it.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Don't see yours? Get in touch and we'll answer it.

Is Quipu an insurance company?

No. Quipu works alongside your insurance. We help you compare prices before care and check the bill after, and our advocates deal with the hospital and insurer for you.

What is an EOB, and why do you need it?

An Explanation of Benefits is the statement your insurer sends after a claim. It shows what was billed, what the plan allowed and paid, and your share. Comparing it with the provider's bill is how most errors are found.

Should I pay my bill while you review it?

Usually not. Once a dispute is open we ask the provider to hold the account. If you receive a collections notice, send it to your advocate right away.

Does Nia make decisions on my case?

No. Nia and the background agents read documents and prepare drafts. A human advocate reviews and approves every email and fax before it's sent.

Which services can I compare prices for?

Common shoppable care: mammograms, X-rays, MRI and CT scans, ultrasounds, blood work and clinic visits. We're adding more services and locations over time.

How do we know a finding is right?

Every fact links back to the page it came from, and the checks that decide a finding are fixed rules, not AI guesses. Results are split into what checked out and what didn't, problems are listed rather than hidden, and Quipu records exactly which documents each result used. Approved actions run in your own systems.

What if the member already paid?

Quipu checks payments against the payer's payment record and the member's receipts, not the provider's portal, which is often out of date. When a member has overpaid, the case is about a refund, and Quipu drafts that request too.
