01 · Story

Named for a record that keeps every account

A quipu is the knotted-cord record the Inca used to keep accounts. Quipus keeps every member's case the same way: every bill, every document, every action, accounted for.

A quipu (also written khipu) recorded taxes, harvests and census counts. A main cord held a row of hanging cords, and knots tied along each one stored the numbers. Trained record-keepers could read a whole ledger from one bundle of string.

Quipus is the plural. It suits a workspace that keeps many accounts at once. Say it "kee-poo": the final s is silent but always written.

Use the idea, not the history lesson. It supports lines about keeping account, like "every step on the record" and "every thread accounted for".

A woven lattice: two cords each way, with a knot tied at every crossing. A small gap is cut in the cords around each knot so it reads as tied on, and two of the knots sit lighter.

What Quipus is, and isn't

Quipus is

Software that reads, checks and drafts: bill advocacy after the visit, care finding before it. A business product for advocacy teams.

Quipus isn't

A TPA, an insurer or a claims processor. A replacement for advocates. A consumer app: members never sign up, they hear from your team.

03 · Voice

Rules

  • Write Quipus, always with the s. Never "Quipu", "QUIPUS" or "Quipus AI".
  • Nia is the assistant. Refer to Nia as "it". Nia prepares; it never decides and never sends on its own.
  • Say member, not patient. Say case for the work. Say the plan, not "your insurance".
  • Sentence case for headings, buttons and labels. No small uppercase labels above section headlines, and no full stop at the end of a headline, label, button or caption.
  • Don't name the advocate. Say "your advocate" or "your team". Named people in examples confuse readers; members in lists and inboxes are fine.
  • No "Coming soon" tags and no "AI" chips. Only describe what the product does today.
  • Money always with cents: $1,240.00, never $1,240 or $1.2k.
  • We is the Quipus team; you, your team, your advocates is the customer.
  • No exclamation marks, no emoji, no invented statistics. Sample data is labelled as sample data ("Illustrative case").
04 · Messaging

Lead with capacity, control and accuracy

Savings are a result we can mention, never the lead. Every page, deck and email opens with one of these three.

Capacity

The reading, the ledger and the first draft are done on every case. Work continues overnight, so the morning starts with drafts, not with an inbox.

Control

Advocates stay in charge. Nothing goes out without sign-off, and every step is on the record: who, when, and from which document.

Accuracy

Findings come from fixed rules and link to their source page and line. Problems are listed, never hidden.

06 · Colour

White space, one ink, one blue

Quipus is a light brand. Blue carries the brand; status colours only ever mean a state and always come with a word. Click a swatch to copy its hex.

One place for every colour. All colours are defined in the :root block of assets/css/site.css (and version2/site.css for the indigo variant). Pages, inline markup and scripts use variables only. To re-theme, change the values there.

Rough proportions on a page: mostly white and band, ink for text, blue for action, status colours in small doses.

09 · Motion

Every motion is eased. Nothing snaps

Movement is slow enough to follow and never jumps. Shadows, heights and positions change together. Press play to compare the curves.

  • 0.4s for colour and opacity on hover. 0.8s for card lift and shadow. 1.1s for sections rising into view.
  • Animated product scenes (Nia's cursor, the overnight queue, the member's phone, the night-to-sunrise card) play only while on screen, and ease to a stop when you scroll away.
  • With reduced motion on: no lifts or loops, fades only.
  • On phones, taps give a light haptic tick, and the browser's tap highlight is off.

13 · Accessibility

Readable for everyone, on every screen

  • Text meets 4.5:1 contrast. The exception is ink-3 meta text (2.9:1), kept from the product: never put anything essential in it.
  • Focus is a solid 2px blue outline with a 3px offset.
  • Status is never colour alone; it always has a word or icon.
  • Phone layouts are designed for the phone: grids become stacks, tabs become tap grids, nothing scrolls sideways.
  • Reduced motion removes lifts and loops but keeps the content.
